About Mike

Built on Honesty & Craft

Hi, I'm Mike C., founder of Right Choice Locksmith.

Locksmithing has been part of my life since I was a kid. I grew up learning the trade alongside my father and grandfather, who taught me that great service is built on honesty, reliability, and treating every customer with respect.

After years of experience, I started Right Choice Locksmith to provide dependable locksmith services with fair pricing, fast response times, and quality workmanship. Whether you need help with your home, business, or vehicle, my goal is to make the process as simple and stress-free as possible.

When you call Right Choice Locksmith, you're working directly with me, not a large call center or national dispatch service. I take pride in showing up on time, communicating clearly, and making sure every job is done right the first time.
