Car Key Types

We Replace Most Types of Car Keys

The type of key your vehicle uses determines the equipment and process required. When you call, tell us your year, make, and model — we'll confirm what's needed before we arrive.

💡

Transponder / Chip Keys

Most vehicles made after the mid-1990s use a transponder key with an embedded chip that communicates with the vehicle's immobilizer. We cut the blade and program the chip to match your vehicle on-site.

🔑

Smart Keys & Push-to-Start

Push-to-start vehicles use a proximity smart key. We supply and program replacement smart keys on-site for most makes and models, including situations where no working key is present. See our key fob programming service →

✂️

Laser-Cut & High-Security Keys

Some vehicles — including many European makes and certain domestic models — use a laser-cut or sidewinder blade. We carry laser-cutting equipment for on-site service.

🗝️

Standard Cut Keys

Older vehicles without a transponder use a mechanically-cut key. These are typically the quickest and most straightforward replacement.

All Keys Lost

Lost All Your Keys? We Handle It.

Losing every key to a vehicle is more complex than cutting a spare — the vehicle's immobilizer needs to be reprogrammed, not just a new key cut. We carry the programming equipment for all-keys-lost situations for most makes and models. Call us with your year, make, and model and we'll confirm compatibility and pricing before we arrive.

🔧

All-Keys-Lost Programming

When no working key is present, we connect to your vehicle's system and program a new key from scratch. This requires specialized equipment beyond standard key cutting.

  • On-site programming
  • Mobile service
  • Requires proof of ownership
  • Call to confirm compatibility
✂️

Spare Key While You Still Have One

If you have a working key and need a spare cut and programmed, this is the most straightforward situation. We cut and program the spare on-site in a single visit.

  • Key cut and programmed on-site
  • Most makes and models
  • Quick turnaround
  • 24/7 availability

Replacement via VIN or Key Code

In many cases, your vehicle's key code can be looked up by VIN, allowing us to cut a replacement blade without a working key present. Proof of ownership is required.

  • VIN-based key cutting
  • Proof of ownership required
  • Available for many makes and models
  • Combined with on-site programming

Before You Call

What to Have Ready When You Call

🚗

Vehicle Year, Make, and Model

This tells us what equipment and key blanks to bring. Different vehicles require different hardware — having this ready speeds up the call and our preparation.

📋

Your VIN

Your VIN is on the driver-side dashboard (visible through the windshield) or on your registration. Having it ready speeds up compatibility confirmation and, in some cases, allows key code lookup.

📄

Proof of Ownership

For all-keys-lost situations and VIN-based key cutting, we require proof that you own the vehicle. Your title, registration, or insurance card showing your name and the VIN is sufficient.
