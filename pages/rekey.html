How Rekeying Works

What Happens During a Rekey

🔩

The Cylinder Comes Out

Rekeying doesn't replace your lock hardware. A locksmith removes the lock cylinder from your existing deadbolt or handle — your hardware stays exactly where it is.

📌

New Pins Go In

The internal pins inside the cylinder are replaced with a new set matched to a different key cut. Old keys will no longer turn the lock — only the new keys will.

✂️

New Keys Cut On-Site

We bring a key-cutting machine so your new keys are cut and tested during the same visit. You leave with working keys and a lock only you can open.
